Its endpoint/matrix checks are consistency checks; the standards, algebra and input assumptions are the independent justification.

For gamma g=2.4, BT.1886 gives L=a·max(V+b,0)^g, a=(Lw^(1/g)−Lb^(1/g))^g, b=Lb^(1/g)/(Lw^(1/g)−Lb^(1/g)). L, Lb and Lw are luminances in cd/m²; V is dimensionless signal normalised to black 0 and white 1. For 0≤V≤1 and 0≤Lb<Lw this is equivalently [V·(Lw^(1/g)−Lb^(1/g))+Lb^(1/g)]^g. Only Lb=0 reduces to Lw·V^2.4. BT.709 camera OETF and BT.1886 display EOTF are distinct.
