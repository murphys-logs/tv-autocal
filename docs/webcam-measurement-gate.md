# Design proposal: webcam gate for patch measurements

**Proposal by Codex (OpenAI); not implemented or tested.** The requirement and working rules are [D-008 and D-009, widened to the whole screen by D-010 and conditioned by D-011, in the ledger](../LEDGER.md#3-decisions), inherited from merged PRs #6, #7 and #8. The owner's original words and follow-up answers remain in [S-009](briefs/2026-10-24-webcam-menu-guard.md), [S-010](briefs/2026-10-24-whole-screen-menu-guard.md) and [S-011](briefs/2026-10-25-meter-placement.md). This document adds a design, not another record of the requirement. The proposed gate is C-017; its timing and end-to-end reliability remain unproven (C-018 and C-019). C-024 is not tested, so the area hidden by the meter currently prevents a Clear state.

## Relationship to D-008, D-009, D-010 and D-011

D-008, as widened by D-010, defines which observations must be excluded. D-009 defines the six working rules below, applied to the whole screen under D-010. D-011 permits ignoring the screen hidden by the meter, its cap and its cable only if T-010 confirms C-024: any menu covering that area is also visible somewhere the webcam can see at the same time. Until then, or if the test fails, the hidden area is Unknown and no reading is accepted. No change to these decisions is proposed; the additions in this document need review as implementation choices. A menu anywhere on the TV screen matters even if it is outside the colour meter's patch. T-010 checks the view, the actual hidden area and where and how menus appear with the meter hung in place but not measuring. D-011 settles how the hidden area is treated; no new owner question is needed. Q-008 retains only the pre-reading clear duration. What counts as a menu remains open under Q-010; this proposal does not settle that definition.

| D-009 item | How this proposal applies it | Added detail or difference |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fail safe | Only a reliable Clear state permits a new reading; Menu visible and Unknown block or invalidate it. A camera picture too dark or too bright to judge is Unknown. | Adds explicit states, timestamp/coverage and exposure checks, alignment checks and a prohibition on bypassing the gate during profiling. Applies D-011 to the area hidden by the meter, its cap and its cable. |
| 2. | 3. Seeing, not assuming | Use webcam observations to decide visibility. Sending no TV command is never evidence that the screen is clear. | Adds image-space alignment and detector validation. A patch change or TV adjustment resets settling as a precaution; it never proves Clear. |

## Intended measurement flow

Do not start a patch reading while a menu is visible anywhere on the TV screen. If one appears at any point during the reading, reject the entire reading: it must never enter the profile, calibration calculations, optimiser or exported valid measurements. After the menu disappears, take a completely new reading once the view is reliably clear and settled. An invalid reading cannot become valid again.

The webcam supplies the menu/visibility check. The intended colour measurements still come from the Spyder5. Webcam frames must continue to be examined while a menu is present so the program can know when it clears; those monitoring frames are not colour-meter patch readings.

## Proposed behaviour

Classify the monitored view as **Clear**, **Menu visible** or **Unknown**. Only Clear can permit a new reading. A missing, stale or unreliable camera view is Unknown, never assumed clear. Profiling must not proceed while this gate is disabled, unavailable or bypassed.

1. **Establish the view.** Locate the entire TV screen and assess its visibility under D-010 and D-011. Use TV-screen coordinates within the webcam image. The area behind the meter, its cap and its cable is Unknown unless T-010 confirms C-024 for the current setup. Only then may that recorded hidden area be ignored when deciding Clear; all other screen areas must remain reliably visible, and menus visible elsewhere still block or invalidate readings. Record the area found by T-010 rather than assuming a mask from the approximate meter dimensions or the unverified cable route in C-023. If the camera, meter, cap or cable moves so the recorded area or supporting evidence no longer applies, return to Unknown until the setup is restored or revalidated. A crop must not exclude any part of the TV screen; other missing or obscured areas are Unknown. Camera setup remains Q-007; detection and timing acceptance criteria are Q-009.
2. **Wait before starting.** Require fresh, reliable Clear observations throughout a settling interval before starting the meter. A menu, unknown state, patch change or TV adjustment resets that interval. The settling duration remains Q-008; this proposal chooses no value.
3. **Monitor throughout acquisition.** Continue observation for the complete time over which the meter could be collecting light, using conservative timing bounds if exact integration times are unavailable. On a menu or unknown state, request a safe cancellation if supported. If cancellation is unavailable or cannot be confirmed, let the outstanding read finish and discard its result; do not start an overlapping retry. Rejection cannot undo light already collected, so no contaminated result may be used.
4. **Hold results provisionally.** A meter result must wait until the corresponding camera interval has been processed. Use frame capture/exposure times, with uncertainty bounds, rather than delivery time alone. A menu frame delivered late but captured during acquisition invalidates that reading. If timing or delivery bounds cannot be established, the result remains ineligible; reaching a deadline discards it rather than assuming success.
5. **Recover with a fresh read.** Give each acquisition its own identifier. Once rejected, associate any late meter result with that rejected acquisition and discard it. Wait for a new uninterrupted Clear settling interval, then begin a fresh acquisition for that patch. Lost screen alignment or an obstructed camera requires correction before resuming. Keep rejected readings out of valid datasets and optimisation. Log every blocked or discarded reading with its acquisition/attempt identifier, time and reason; record the affected interval and recovery separately without storing room imagery by default.

Camera disconnects, frozen/stale frames, unexplained frame gaps, out-of-order frames that cannot be accounted for, unbounded latency, loss of screen alignment, occlusion not covered by the confirmed D-011 condition, uncertain classifications, and a camera picture too dark or too bright to judge must block or invalidate readings. Camera exposure changes make the view Unknown until usable visibility and reliable capture timing/coverage are re-established; recovery requires a fresh Clear settling interval. This concerns the camera's ability to see menus, including during exposure transitions, separately from whether the displayed test patch is legitimately dark or bright. These are proposed failure conditions; reliable detection of them must itself be verified. A static patch alone must not be treated as proof of a frozen camera.

The user-facing status should explain the pause plainly, for example “Waiting for the TV menu to disappear” or “Webcam view unavailable — measurement paused.” Detecting a menu does not itself trigger a TV command or attempt to dismiss it. No service-menu action is part of this feature.

## Timing and detection limits

The owner's “at any point” requirement covers the full acquisition interval. A webcam samples frames; it cannot be assumed to observe an arbitrarily short event between exposures. A visually missed menu can also be wrongly classified as Clear. Synthetic tests cannot establish that neither problem occurs on the owner's setup.

Before claiming the feature meets the requirement, validate whole-screen coverage under D-010 and D-011, camera frame/exposure coverage, exposure behaviour and changes, latency, clock alignment, minimum detectable event duration and false-negative behaviour against representative TV menus and patch conditions. Check under-exposed and over-exposed camera pictures, exposure transitions and recovery, and whether exposure changes affect frame spacing or timing bounds; none of that behaviour is established for this webcam. Include menus across the top, middle, bottom, sides, edges and corners, including outside the meter's patch, and menus covering the recorded hidden area that must also be visible elsewhere under C-024. The hidden-area exception requires T-010 evidence for the applicable setup and menu coverage; a few synthetic examples cannot confirm C-024. Bright, dark or changing patches, translucent bars and partial bars must also be included, separately from unusable camera pictures. Do not quietly reinterpret the requirement as “no menu in the occasional frames we checked.” An unvalidated or inadequate observation interval must not qualify a reading as valid. If the available webcam cannot support the required assurance, record that limitation and keep the capability unverified rather than claiming complete protection. The additional timing, exposure and detection limits and acceptance criteria remain Q-009; Q-008 retains only the pre-reading clear duration. This uncertainty adds a validation obligation to D-009; it does not grant an exception beyond D-011 to D-008 as widened by D-010.

## Proposed software-only checks — T-011

**Planned; not executed.** Use synthetic frames/events, an in-memory fake meter and a fake clock. No camera, TV, colour meter, sockets or device enumeration is needed. Test both the classifier's handling of labelled synthetic images and the measurement gate's response to scripted classifications; passing the latter alone does not validate menu detection.

Represent C-024's unconfirmed, failed and confirmed states as simulated test inputs. A simulated confirmation is never evidence from T-010 and never changes C-024's equipment test status. Cases that permit a simulated reading must explicitly supply the confirmed case for any meter-hidden area.

| Case | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| Menu visible before a reading at the top, middle, bottom, sides, edges or corners, including outside the patch | No meter-start request for any tested position. |
| Clear, valid coverage across settling and the complete acquisition interval | Result eligible only after the camera interval is processed; eligibility is labelled simulated. |
| Menu appears briefly midway through acquisition at any of the screen positions above and disappears before the result | Entire result rejected for every tested position; no profile, optimiser or valid-export update. |
| Menu touches the beginning or end of the acquisition interval | Treat overlap conservatively and reject; exercise timing uncertainty bounds too. |
| Menu frame captured during acquisition arrives after the meter result | Provisional result rejected before any consumer can use it. |
| Camera disconnects, timestamps stop advancing, or coverage contains an unexplained gap | Unknown state blocks/invalidates and pauses further readings. |
| The meter, its cap or its cable hides screen area and simulated T-010 evidence for C-024 is absent, inconclusive or failed | Unknown; no meter-start request, and any active reading is rejected. Do not ignore the hidden area. |
| Simulated T-010 confirmation of C-024 applies to the current setup and recorded meter/cap/cable hidden area | The recorded hidden area alone no longer blocks Clear; all other visibility, settling and timing checks still apply. A menu covering that area and simultaneously visible elsewhere blocks or rejects the reading. Any eligibility is simulated only. |
| TV/camera/meter/cap/cable movement makes alignment or the recorded hidden area/evidence inapplicable; a crop excludes screen area; another area is obscured; or detector is uncertain | Unknown; block a new reading or invalidate an active reading. Include upper-screen, edge and corner coverage failures. |
| Camera picture is too dark or too bright to judge, before or during acquisition | Unknown; do not start a new reading and reject any active reading. A legitimate dark/bright patch does not excuse an unusable camera picture. |
| Camera exposure changes before or during acquisition, including transitions that alter image usability or frame spacing/timing bounds | Unknown; block or invalidate until usable visibility and valid timing/coverage are re-established, then require fresh Clear settling before a new attempt. |
| Menu clears but reappears during settling | Settling restarts; the rejected reading remains rejected. |
| Meter cannot cancel, or returns after cancellation | Finish/drain and discard; no overlap with retry and no attribution to a later reading. |
| Translucent/partial bars and bright/dark patches in synthetic labelled images | A menu label blocks measurement; uncertain cases are Unknown. Record misses explicitly, not as passing gate tests. |
| User attempts to disable the gate during profiling | Profiling cannot proceed with unguarded readings. |
| Timing/delivery bounds are unavailable, frames arrive out of order, or the provisional-result deadline expires | Unaccounted coverage remains Unknown; discard on the deadline, never accept by timeout. |
| A static patch produces identical images but timestamps and coverage remain valid | Do not diagnose a frozen camera solely from repeated image content. |
| A reading is blocked or discarded and recovery creates a new attempt | Log the time, reason and correct attempt identifier; retain no frame by default. |

Record the implementation revision, fixture provenance, clock assumptions, thresholds and actual passed/failed/skipped/unrun outcomes when these tests exist. No suite or detector has been implemented by this documentation change.

## Equipment, privacy and later validation

The owner already has a webcam (C-016). Its model, connection and placement remain Q-007; suitability remains untested (C-015 and C-019). No purchase is proposed and the hardware budget is unchanged. Process frames locally by default and do not retain or upload room images or video by default. If frames are deliberately retained for investigation, they stay in `local/` under D-009 item 5 and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#11-privacy-and-credentials). The owner must check and approve each specific picture before it is committed. No agent turns the webcam on or captures pictures outside an authorised test plan (D-009 item 6).

Main's T-010 remains the candidate physical test for the whole-screen camera view, menu locations, the area hidden by the meter/cap/cable and C-024 under D-010 and D-011. The Spyder5 is hung in place but takes no reading in T-010; the test has no plan or authorisation. Actual detector performance and meter-timing integration need a later, explicitly authorised equipment-validation plan (T-012), building on those findings. T-012 must retain D-011's prerequisite for permitting meter readings. It would assess capture timing, latency and clock bounds; unusably dark/bright camera pictures, exposure changes and recovery, including any effect on frame spacing; the representative menu/patch conditions and whole-screen positions above; and blocking, discard and fresh-read recovery across real acquisition windows. Its steps and acceptance criteria must be written and reviewed before authorisation; none is written or authorised here. Software-only T-011 can proceed without it, but cannot confirm C-018, C-019 or C-024. A future full calibration plan under T-008 must also satisfy D-008 and D-009 as widened by D-010 and conditioned by D-011 and account for the applicable webcam checks; this proposal does not authorise that test.
